03

Machine learning · Public health

Water potability model

A comparative classification study using measurable chemical properties to estimate whether water is safe for human consumption—and documenting where the model still falls short.

3,276water samples
3 modelscompared consistently
67.4%best test accuracy
Method
Median imputation, an 80/20 split, scaling for linear models, and feature-importance analysis.
Finding
Random Forest performed best; sulfate, pH, and hardness were the leading individual predictors.
Next test
Cross-validation, hyperparameter tuning, and class-imbalance treatment are documented as the next steps.
